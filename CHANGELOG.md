**Added:**
*Reputation from duels: this depends on realm only for now.*

Lower realm wins: the winner gains 25 reputation per realm of difference, and the loser loses 20 per realm.
Righteous vs Righteous: the loser only loses 10 per realm.
Righteous vs Demonic, Demonic vs Righteous and Demonic vs Demonic: the loser loses the full 20 per realm.
Same realm: nothing changes.
Stronger realm wins: nothing changes either.
Example: a Core Formation cultivator beating a Soul Formation one is a 1-realm gap, so the winner gets +25 and the loser gets -20 (or -10 if both are Righteous).

**changes:**

1.Dead cultivators: "mocb" now refuses a duel if either cultivator died in their fate. It says who died, in which year and at what age, and that the dead cannot duel. Nothing is changed in that case: no loot, no reputation, no battle output. If both are dead, both are named. The program keeps running and the log records it as an error.

2.Outage threshold: it is now 12 instead of 5. A scan only counts as a Steam outage when 12 or more games are asked and all of them fail.
