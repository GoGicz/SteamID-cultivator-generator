**Added:**
*Battle arsenal*

Each cultivator keeps their signature technique (named after their most played game), and it still starts the first clash and delivers the deciding blow.

Each cultivator also gets four more moves, fixed by their SteamID: two attacks of their own element, one attack of their path, and one defense or movement technique. 

These come from premade lists (54 element attacks, 10 path attacks, 8 defenses), and this doesn’t change anything else about an existing cultivator.

The battle story has a new middle section where both fighters use these moves. It has different wording for close fights, clear wins, crushing wins and upsets, and it picks one or two lines at random each time. The templates only call the moves “an attack” or “a defense”, so any combination reads sensibly.

The arsenal shows on the cultivator card as Arsenal and in each duel fighter block as Moves, wrapped between names so no name is split.
